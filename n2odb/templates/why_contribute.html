Why contribute?

Your measurements are hard-won. Sharing them multiplies their impact — and it takes a guided upload, not a spreadsheet battle.

Why it matters

GRAND Explorer is an open, harmonized repository for agricultural N2O flux data. The measurements the field needs already exist — they're just scattered across hundreds of papers in incompatible units and structures. Every dataset you add is one more piece pulled into a single, clean, queryable whole that the whole community can build on.

Pooled, covariate-rich data is exactly what modern emission models and meta-analyses are starving for. Your contribution doesn't just sit in a table — it strengthens the science everyone downstream depends on.

What you get

Amplified reach

Your data becomes discoverable and reusable alongside work from labs worldwide, extending the life of the effort you already invested.

Better models

Harmonized flux data powers the process-based and machine-learning models that individual studies can't support alone.

An assisted upload

Attach your paper and our workbench pre-fills site, soil, and management details for you — you review and confirm, not retype.

Real recognition

Contributors are credited and ranked on our leaderboard, so your generosity is visible to the community.

How it works

  1. Upload your data. Drop in a CSV or Excel file of measurements — and, optionally, the paper it came from.
  2. Review the pre-fill. We read your file and paper to suggest columns, site details, and management; you confirm or adjust.
  3. Submit for curation. A curator checks it over, and once approved it's published into the live database — credited to you.
