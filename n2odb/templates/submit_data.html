Contribute your data

How to complete this submission

Plan for one study at a time. You can save your progress and return later; nothing is published until a curator reviews and approves your submission.

Before you start
  • Prepare a CSV or XLSX file with one measurement observation per row and a valid date column. Repeated dates are allowed for replicates or multiple measurements within a day; the workbench will organize them into daily values.
  • In addition to the mandatory date column, include the relevant measurement columns and a treatment column if your file contains multiple treatments.
  • Fill in each row with the observed measurements you have for that observation. Values should be measured rather than interpolated or estimated. It is okay to leave some columns blank if you do not have a value for that date.
  • Excel workbooks may contain multiple sheets, but only ONE sheet can contain the primary time-series data. Other sheets can contain metadata, static site data (for example, texture or climate), or treatment information. CSV files contain one sheet.
  • Have the source paper ready, if available.
What happens next
  1. Upload and preview your measurements as a CSV or Excel file. Optionally add your paper text, PDF, or DOCX for AI-assisted extraction of study details.
  2. Map columns to the database fields.
  3. Confirm study, site, soil, and management details.
  4. Add dates and management events, then review.
  5. Submit for curator review.
Paper assistance

A PDF, DOCX, or pasted methods/site description can pre-fill DOI, location, crop, soil, management details, and events. The assistant does not replace your measurement data or validate every scientific value. Suggestions are only a starting point: check every value and correct anything that is wrong.

If something is missing

You can continue without a paper or without AI assistance. Fill in the forms manually, use the progress rail to revisit completed stages, and use the preview to confirm that the data file was read correctly.
